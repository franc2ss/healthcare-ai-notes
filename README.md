# Healthcare AI Notes

Open-source study notes and resources related to healthcare artificial intelligence and biomedical engineering.

## About

This repository contains learning materials and summaries related to:

- Healthcare AI
- Biomedical engineering
- Medical data analysis
- AI-assisted medical devices
- Digital healthcare technologies
- Clinical AI applications

## Purpose

The goal of this project is to organize beginner-friendly study resources for students interested in healthcare technology and medical artificial intelligence.

## Planned Contents

- AI in medical devices
- Healthcare data examples
- Biomedical engineering notes
- Digital health concepts
- Regulatory considerations for AI medical devices

## Topics

- Deep Learning
- Medical Imaging AI
- Biosignal Processing
- Clinical Decision Support Systems
